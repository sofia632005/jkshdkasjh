My POV

“Criticism is not a verdict; it is an invitation to listen closer to what the text leaves unsaid.”

I believe books are living archives that talk back to us when we pay attention to their mechanics. In an age of rapid consumption, close reading offers a counter-practice: slowing down to examine the friction between authorial intent, historical pressure, and readerly imagination.
